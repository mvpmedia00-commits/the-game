# MVP Media: Las Vegas

*Own the frame.*

A third-person, open-world crime-and-hustle game set in a fictional, after-hours Las Vegas. You play **Matthew "MVP" Phillips**, a photographer in a red beret and red suit. He starts with a borrowed lens and room 12 at the Desert Rose Motel and builds **MVP Media** into the biggest studio on the Strip.

The game is about the business of nightlife: club promos, magazine covers, red carpets and film production. It is played through photography, driving and deal-making.

- **Rating intent:** mature themes, non-explicit. Productions cut to "That's a wrap." Every performer is a named adult professional with a contract and a paid day rate.
- **Platform:** plays in the browser today (Chrome, Safari, iPhone). Targeted at Steam.

---

## 1. The pitch in one paragraph

It's night on Las Vegas Blvd. Your phone rings: Tony Velvet needs marquee shots of his headliner, Ruby Monroe, and he needs them before midnight. From there, every job you take is a step up:
- Shoot a club set.
- Race proofs to a magazine editor.
- Chase down a paparazzo who stole your memory cards.
- Land a swimwear cover.
- Buy an old soundstage.
- Produce your first film.
- Shut down a bootlegger.
- Shoot the premiere of your own movie, while the rival who has been pirating you, Silas Crane, makes one last grab for the master drive.

## 3. The world

The playable area is the crossing of **Las Vegas Blvd** (north–south, about 600 m) and **Fremont Ave** (east–west, about 500 m). It's 11:40 PM and the streets are wet.

### Landmarks

**Crown Casino (north-west)**
- A 104 m gold tower topped with a slowly turning crown, and a porte-cochère glowing with bulbs.
- A lit fountain in the forecourt.
- The editor waits at the entrance.
- For the finale, a red carpet with an MVP MEDIA step-and-repeat wall appears here.

**The Velvet Room (north-east)**
- Red neon, a velvet rope line, a bouncer, and an MVP MEDIA billboard on the roof.
- You can **walk inside**: a velvet-curtained stage with LED rings, sweeping spotlights you can see in the haze, a bar with backlit bottle shelves, red booths, and a dancing crowd.

**Lens & Light and Scarlet Lounge (north-east)**
- Lens & Light has cameras in the window and Dee at the counter.
- Scarlet Lounge is a pink-neon burlesque bar. Coco stands at the door.

**Desert Rose Motel (south-west)**
- Two floors of numbered rooms around a glowing night pool, with loungers and palms.
- A tall pole sign reads "DESERT ROSE · MOTEL · POOL · VACANCY".
- **Room 12** is home and a save point.

**MVP Studios (south-east)**
- A warehouse with a red LED sign and a roll-up door. Your red car is parked out front.
- **Locked until you buy it** in mission 5.
- Inside is **Stage A**: a gray cyclorama backdrop, two softboxes, red LED tubes, MVP MEDIA road cases, a lens cart and a laptop station.

**Also on the streets**
- The Chapel of Love, the Diamond Diner, and about 45 other neon storefronts along the extended strip.
- A palm-lined median and streetlights.
- Traffic lights that cycle: the boulevard is green for 14 s, yellow for 3 s, then Fremont gets its turn.

### Life on the street

- **Traffic:** about 34 cars stay in their lanes, stop at red lights, queue behind each other, and brake for you.
- **Pedestrians:** about 30 people walk the sidewalks. They step around you and jump out of the way of your car.
- **Clock:** time runs from about 11:40 PM onward. Each in-game day pays your film royalties.

## 4. How you play

### On foot
- Third-person camera over MVP's shoulder.
- Walk, or run with Shift (or push the joystick all the way).
- Walls stop the camera so it never clips through buildings.
- Press **E** to talk, enter doors, shop or save.

### Driving
- Get in the red MVP car with **F**.
- The car accelerates, brakes, reverses and has a handbrake. Steering tightens at low speed and is calmer at high speed.
- Hitting a wall bounces you back. Hitting another car stops it.
- A chase camera follows behind and widens with speed, with a speedometer in MPH and an engine sound that rises with speed.
- Top speed is about 72 mph, and Dee's Engine Tune adds more.

### Photography (the core skill)
- Press **SHOOT** (or P) at any time to take a photo. It lands in the strip at the bottom of the screen.
- During a **photo job**, a rule-of-thirds grid and a "● REC" indicator appear, and each frame is graded from A+ to D:
  - **Composition (45%):** is her face on one of the four thirds points?
  - **Framing (20%):** does she fill enough of the frame?
  - **Timing (35%):** did you catch the peak of the pose? Models hold each pose for a moment; dancers hit the beat.
  - **Gear bonus:** each gimbal and lens upgrade adds +4, and each lighting kit adds +3.
- After the last shot you get a **contact sheet** with real thumbnails of your frames, their grades, the average, and a **quality bonus**:
  - An A average pays $200 plus 4 × your rep, and adds +2 rep.
  - A B average pays $80.

### The phone (Tab, M, or the PHONE button)
- **Story:** all 8 missions, which are done, and who to see next.
- **Photo gigs:** three freelance jobs a day at the pool, the casino forecourt, under the billboard or the chapel. Some are timed rush jobs.
- **Talent roster:** everyone you've signed and who is still available.
- **Studio productions:** run once you own the studio and are inside it.
- **Save game.**

### Markers
- A **red beacon** marks your current objective.
- On the circular minimap: gold dots are places of interest, a red dot is MVP Studios, a red square is your parked car, and a pulsing ring is your target.

## 5. The story: 8 missions

| # | Mission | Starts with | What you do | Reward |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Velvet Hours** | Phone call from Tony Velvet | Walk to The Velvet Room, go inside, shoot Ruby's headliner set (4 photos) | $900, +8 rep, **Ruby signs** |
| 2 | **Proof Run** | Tony at the club door | Get the proofs to the editor at Crown Casino within **75 s** (drive) | $600, +6 rep |
| 3 | **Snatch & Grab** | Editor at Crown Casino | A Neon Kings coupe stole your memory cards. **Chase and ram it** until its health bar empties | $1,000, +10 rep |
| 4 | **Pool Party Cover** | Dee at Lens & Light | Reach the Desert Rose pool within **120 s**, shoot Nova (5 photos) | $1,500, +10 rep, **Nova signs** |
| 5 | **Keys to the Kingdom** | Landlord at Room 12 | Buy the soundstage at MVP Studios for **$3,500** | +10 rep, **you own the studio** |
| 6 | **First Feature** | Jade outside the studio | Go inside and produce your first film with Jade | +12 rep, **Jade signs** |
| 7 | **Bootleg Boulevard** | Coco at Scarlet Lounge | Your film is being bootlegged. **Ram the bootleg van** on Fremont | $2,500, +12 rep, **Coco signs** |
| 8 | **Beyond the Frame** | Ruby outside the studio | Reach the premiere at Crown Casino within **90 s**, shoot the red carpet with Ruby, Nova and Jade (6 photos), then **stop Silas Crane's car** | $5,000, +20 rep, ending |

**Chases:**
- The getaway driver follows the real roads. At the main intersection it turns whichever way takes it farthest from you.
- Each ram does damage based on your speed and closing speed.
- If you fall more than 230 m behind for 8 seconds, they escape and you can retry.

**Failing:** running out of time or losing a chase gives you **Retry** or **Later**. Nothing is lost.

**After the finale:** the credits roll, and the city keeps going with gigs, productions and royalties.

## 6. The business

### Money and reputation
- You start with **$250** and **0 rep**. Missions, gigs and quality bonuses pay cash.
- Rep (0–100) raises gig pay, film sales and photo bonuses, and unlocks talent.

### Photo gigs
- Three a day from the phone.
- Pay = $250 + 12 × rep + a random tip.
- Rush gigs give you 90 seconds to arrive.
- A freelance model (all adults) waits at the location and poses for 4 photos.

### Dee's gear shop (Lens & Light)

| Item | Effect | Price (doubles roughly 1.8× per level) |
|---|---|---|
| Gimbal Stabilizer | +4 photo score (3 levels) | from $400 |
| Fast Prime Lens | +4 photo score (3 levels) | from $500 |
| Lighting Kit | +3 photo score and better film quality (3 levels) | from $600 |
| Engine Tune | +4 m/s top speed (2 levels) | from $700 |

### Talent roster

| Performer | Stars | Day rate | Likes | How to sign |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Ruby Monroe | 3 | $600 | neon light, playful | Mission 1 |
| Nova Starr | 3 | $700 | warm light, romantic | Mission 4 |
| Jade Rivers | 5 | $1,500 | cool light, dramatic | Mission 6 |
| Coco Bellamy | 2 | $400 | warm light, playful | Mission 7, or 25 rep + $800 |
| Alexis "Lex" Vaughn | 4 | $1,100 | neon light, dramatic | 45 rep + $2,200 |

### Studio productions
You can make **one per in-game day**, inside MVP Studios.

1. **Cast** up to 2 signed performers. You pay their day rates.
2. **Set:**
   - Stage A (free)
   - Desert Rose pool ($200)
   - Velvet Room after hours ($400)
   - Crown penthouse ($600)
3. **Crew:**
   - Lean (free, ×0.9 quality)
   - Standard ($800, ×1.1)
   - Premium ($2,000, ×1.35)
4. **Director's calls:** choose the lighting, mood and camera. Matching a performer's taste in lighting and mood earns +7 each.
5. **Wrap:** quality comes out of 100.
   - Opening sales = quality × (25 + rep) × 2.2.
   - **Royalties** of 8% of opening sales arrive every day after.

Productions are about the business: casting, sets, lighting, mood, camera and the box office. Scenes end at "Cut! That's a wrap." Each set is a look, and the look is the product.

**The climb:** your rung shows in the HUD and rises with the story: Hired Lens, Vendor, Runner, Byline, Landlord, Producer, Distributor, Mogul.

**Catalog:** the phone lists every release and its daily royalty. Until you win Bootleg Boulevard, Silas's bootlegs eat half your royalties. Lex will only sign once you have released 2 films.

## 8. Look and feel

- **Rendering:** Three.js (WebGL):
  - Bloom on every neon sign, window and headlight, with film-style tone mapping.
  - Wet streets with puddles that reflect the city lights.
  - Clearcoat car paint, soft moonlight shadows that follow you, and night fog with a star field.
- **Characters:** jointed 3D bodies with walk cycles and 7 photo poses. **Spring-bone physics** makes hair, earrings and skirts sway and bounce. Imported Character Creator models also get chest physics automatically.
- **Color:** MVP red, gold and silver, with neon pink, teal and amber on the street.
- **Sound:** synthesized effects (shutter, cash register, phone ring, door, impacts) and an engine note that rises with speed.
